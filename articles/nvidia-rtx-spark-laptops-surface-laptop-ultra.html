Nvidia RTX Spark Laptops Arrive October 16: Surface Laptop Ultra, Full Lineup with Specs and Pricing

Six brands, one superchip: the RTX Spark GB10 lands in Windows laptops and compact desktops this month.

Hardware WireShaman News Desk Oct 12, 2026 · 7 min read

Nvidia's RTX Spark superchip finally goes on sale. Beginning October 16, 2026, RTX Spark-powered Windows laptops from six manufacturers hit retail, led by Microsoft's new Surface Laptop Ultra — with compact desktops following in November. On October 7, Microsoft and Nvidia held a joint launch event in New York, showing off the Surface Laptop Ultra design and preorders went live across Asus, Lenovo, HP, Dell, and Microsoft. Nvidia is also hosting a public launch party in Berlin on the same day the shelves fill.

The RTX Spark is a single Arm-based superchip (the GB10, codenamed N1X) built with MediaTek on TSMC 3nm: a 20-core Arm CPU paired with a Blackwell-based RTX GPU carrying 6,144 CUDA cores (RTX 5070-class) and 5th-gen Tensor cores, joined over NVLink-C2C with up to 128GB of unified memory and roughly 1 petaflop of FP4 AI compute. It is Nvidia's first Arm-based client PC processor for Windows 11 AI PCs.

What the chip changes in practice

  • Local, large-model AI: the unified-memory design means the CPU and GPU share one memory pool up to 128GB — enough to run 120-billion-parameter LLMs locally; the Surface Dev Box advertises over 120B model capacity with CUDA and TensorRT tooling.
  • Unified-memory catch: the GPU is not a discrete card over PCIe, so performance varies sharply by workload versus a desktop RTX 5070 — Nvidia's own claim is "RTX 5070-class," not parity.
  • Workstation + gaming pitch: Microsoft and Nvidia framed the lineup around "reinventing the PC" with Windows AI features; analysts read it best as local-AI workstations with an Nvidia software stack, not a MacBook Pro replacement.
  • Arm on Windows matures: a Grace CPU means a proven datacenter Arm core lineage in a client device, with full Windows 11 Pro compatibility and CUDA — the first time that combination ships in volume client hardware.

The rollout

Laptops arrive retail on October 16, including the Surface Laptop Ultra; compact desktops (Surface RTX Spark Dev Box and partners' boxes) follow in November 2026. Nvidia marked the moment with a free public "RTX Spark Launch Celebration" at XPERION in Saturn's Alexanderplatz 3, Berlin, on October 16. If you're evaluating local AI workloads on Windows, October 16 is the day benchmarks stop being hypothetical — and the Dev Box's $6,000 ask against AMD's $4,000 alternative is the price the entire category's premium will be judged by.

What this means for you

Wait for independent benchmarks before treating “RTX 5070-class” as desktop-GPU parity. The shared-memory design is most compelling for local AI workloads that need more memory than a conventional laptop GPU can provide, while the launch pricing puts these machines firmly in workstation territory.
